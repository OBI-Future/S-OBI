# Paper figures

These images are faithful raster crops of figures from the S-OBI paper:

> Ziqi Li, Zijian Chen, Tingzhu Chen, and Guangtao Zhai. “Beyond Single Character: Evaluating MLLMs for Sentence-Level Oracle Bone Inscription Understanding.” ICIG 2026.

| File | Paper figure | Source page | Description |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `fig1_obi_sources.png` | Fig. 1 | 2 | Sentential rubbings, single-character OBI resources, and synthetic OBI sentences |
| `fig2_construction_pipeline.png` | Fig. 2 | 5 | S-OBI construction pipeline and the three task tiers |
| `fig4_task_case_studies.png` | Fig. 4 | 10 | Semantic-matching and contextual-reasoning cases at different difficulty levels |

The crops were rendered directly from the paper PDF at high resolution with PyMuPDF. No content was redrawn, enhanced, or changed. The paper caption and surrounding page header were excluded from the crops; labels that belong to each figure were retained. These files are documentation illustrations for the repository and are **not additional benchmark data** or evaluation inputs. The original `sentence-OBI.zip` remains the only benchmark data release.

For the full figures, captions, and publication context, please consult the paper cited above.
